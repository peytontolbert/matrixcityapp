Driving controls

Driving inputs pause while settings are open.

Click the road to steer with the mouse. W = throttle, S = brake, Space = handbrake, C = center steering. Esc releases the mouse and brakes. A/D and arrow keys also steer. Shift down through neutral to reverse while stopped.

Standard controllers work automatically: left stick, RT/LT pedals, RB/LB shifts, B handbrake. Other devices need calibration. Center the wheel and release pedals before each binding, then move the indicated control through its full travel. Wheel force feedback is not provided.

Settings and device mappings are saved in this browser.
