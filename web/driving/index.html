Native Assetto setup - no extra launcher

Select your Steam library (or assettocorsa folder), then Documents / Assetto Corsa / cfg. Folder names and your Steam ID are saved to your profile; file access stays on this device and browser. Choose "Allow on every visit" if your browser offers it.

Use the Steam account that owns Assetto. If detected automatically, confirm it matches the account currently signed in to Steam. Required: CSP, the selected track, the 350Z and traffic Prius.

After saving, click "Join with Assetto Corsa" above, then open Steam below. After exiting the game, restore your settings here. The website cannot detect when the game closes.
